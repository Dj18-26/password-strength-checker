          Why this scoring model

          01 · Entropy

          Charset size and length estimate search space. Repeats, sequences, and dictionary words reduce effective entropy far below the theoretical maximum.

          02 · Attack models

          Online throttled logins, unthrottled APIs, slow hashes (bcrypt/argon2), and GPU-accelerated unsalted hashes produce very different time-to-crack.

          03 · Patterns

          Keyboard walks, years, leetspeak, and common base words are first in wordlists. Length without randomness is not a defense.

          04 · Hygiene

          Use a unique passphrase per account, a password manager, and phishing-resistant MFA (passkeys / hardware keys) for high-value systems.

          Pass-O-Meter · Defensive security tooling · Client-side only

          Estimates are educational. Real attackers also use targeted intel, reuse, and credential stuffing.
